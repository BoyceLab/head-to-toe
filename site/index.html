Head-to-toe health survey, redesigned

A proposed replacement for the ClinGen GenomeConnect Health Survey, for children and adults Summary of changesBefore and afterReview the original surveysSuggest a change

Show clinical terms adds the clinical term and code behind each plain-language answer: the Human Phenotype Ontology (HPO) term for each feature with its OMOP standard concept, the Mondo term for a matched diagnosis, and the ECTO code for each exposure. The terms appear under each item on the head-to-toe pages, the seizure and exposure pages, and the Review page; this About page has none, so nothing changes here. It is meant for coordinators, clinicians, and reviewers. People answering the survey can leave it off; the answers are the same either way.
